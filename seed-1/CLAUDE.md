# seed-1: Dust

The first game. An idle game about dust: it accrues on its own, strikes add a little more, units raise the rate, and unlocks open new units and raise production.

## Pillars

Idle/incremental. One screen. A Three.js web game (`docs/specs/final-state.md`, Games). Mobile-first: the screen fits a 375px portrait phone with no sideways scroll, and every control is at least a 44px touch target there (`docs/PLAN.md` §10 decision 56). Numbers go up. Every feature is visible within 60 seconds of play. A session of two minutes is satisfying. All-ages. Procedural or vector art only, in the studio's line style: thick ink outlines, paper ground, the creature fills and the Dust suit magenta as the one accent; never pixel art.

## Layout

- `sim/` is the pure simulation: `createSim(config, seed)`, `step(state, config, dtSeconds)`, `apply(state, config, action)`, the selectors `ratePerSecond`, `costOf`, `availableUnits`, the state hash, and `checkInvariants(log)`. It imports nothing from Three.js or the DOM and never touches the network, the clock or `Math.random`; the only randomness is the mulberry32 state carried in `SimState`. `sim/save.ts` serializes and parses `SimState` for save and resume, with a version in the save wrapper rather than in `SimState`, so the hash `tests/timeline.test.ts` pins is untouched.
- `config/` and `content/` are the config lane: `spawn-table.json` (units, base costs, rates), `unlocks.json` (thresholds and effects) and `strings.json` (the strings the HUD, the shop and the seed offer show, and the tab title). They are data, read at runtime, copied into `dist/` verbatim by the build, and served with `Cache-Control: no-cache`.
- `render/` is the Three.js scene and the DOM around it. No image assets: every mesh is built from geometry in code, and the page's look is `render/style.css` with the site's font, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, bundled from `render/fonts/` (SIL OFL 1.1, `render/fonts/OFL.txt`). It fetches the config and content files at runtime. `render/main.ts` wires one loop: each animation frame steps the sim by wall-clock time, then draws the scene, the HUD and the controls from the frame's clock.
  - `render/game.ts` is the running game, Three-free and DOM-free: one `SimState`, `act` (strike or buy through the sim's `apply`), `tick` (the wall-clock step) and `save`, and listeners for its moments (a strike's gain, a purchase, unlocks opened). The scene, the HUD and the controls all listen to it, so a tap on the pile and the Strike button are the same strike (`tests/game.test.ts`). It saves to localStorage under the key `dust.save` every 5 seconds, when the tab is hidden and on `pagehide`, and `render/main.ts` resumes from that save on load. It steps by wall-clock time, not frame time, so a background tab is credited when it returns; the save records when it was written (`savedAt`), and loading credits the time since. Both go through `awaySeconds` in `sim/save.ts`, clamped to between 0 and 8 hours.
  - `render/scene.ts` is the diorama: a square quarry island seen from above at an angle with an orthographic camera (steeper on a tall screen), framed inside the part of the stage the HUD and the shop leave open. The dust pile in the middle grows with the log of lifetime dust and carries a magenta flag; gatherers walk lanes to it and back with sacks, carts roll on a track around it, mills turn at the right, forges glow and spark at the left, foundries smoke at the back, and a staked plot marks each building not yet built. Each unit is one instanced mesh, capped (`render/world.ts`), and pops in with an overshoot as it is bought. A strike squashes the pile and throws dust, an unlock throws confetti and punches the camera, a welcome back rains dust into the pile. `render/toon.ts` is the line style: toon fills with one shade step and ink outlines drawn as an inverted hull pushed out along smoothed normals in view space, 2 to 3 CSS pixels whatever the scale, from parts merged into one geometry per prop. `render/world.ts` is the Three-free model (slots, caps, the pile's size, the walking lanes, a fixed hash; `tests/world.test.ts`). The renderer caps the pixel ratio at 2 and draws nothing while the tab is hidden.
  - Determinism: the scene and the HUD move only by the frame clock passed in and a fixed hash, never `Math.random` or a CSS animation or transition, and the framing is read in the frame, so the same state at the same page clock draws the same pixels (`e2e/frames.spec.ts`). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing walks, turns, drifts or flies, floaters fade in place and banners appear still.
  - `render/hud.ts` is the HUD over the scene, for the eye only (aria-hidden): the dust total (counting up toward a gain), the rate, the next goal bar (from the last unlock reached to the next, `render/goals.ts`, `tests/goals.test.ts`, and the next gem once every unlock is open), the first step for a new player or why a purchase was refused, the weather chip, "+N" floaters off the pile, a strike streak (drawn only; it adds nothing to the sim), and the banner for each unlock and for a load that credited at least a minute away, which the live region also says ("While you were away: +N dust in 2h 14m").
  - `render/depth.ts` holds the Three-free words for the layers (weather, perks, milestones, the press, the first steps; `tests/depth-render.test.ts`), with their text under `depth` in `content/strings.json`; `render/scene-depth.ts` draws them on the island (hats and pennants, the weather, the gem ring, season flowers).
  - `render/controls.ts` is the shop and the accessible DOM layer: the canvas is `aria-hidden`, and the controls region in the `<main>` landmark holds real buttons for Strike and each available unit, visible over the scene (a bottom sheet on a phone, a side panel on a wide screen, every button at least 44px). Each unit button's accessible name is its full label (name, cost, owned; `aria-disabled` when unaffordable) and it draws an icon (`render/icons.ts`), the cost, the count and a fill for how close it is to affordable. Below them the gems panel once its unlock opens (press the pile, behind a confirm, and one button per perk), the unlock list (earned unlocks as one count, the next three by name) and a polite live region. A press on an unaffordable unit shakes it and says why. `render/a11y.ts` is its Three-free half (labels and the announcer: purchases and unlocks at once, the dust total at most every 10 seconds; `tests/a11y.test.ts`), with its text under `a11y` in `content/strings.json`. `e2e/a11y.spec.ts` plays with the keyboard only and runs axe.
  - Seeds (`docs/specs/final-state.md`, Seeds and instances): `render/seed-code.ts` is the Three-free code `dust:v<saveVersion>:<base64url(save JSON)>` and its link https://play.mobmachine.games/?seed=<code>, decoded through `parseSavedState` so a seed never loads a state a save could not (`tests/seed-code.test.ts`). `render/seed-ui.ts` wires "Save as seed" beside Share your run (the share sheet with the link, else the clipboard), adds a "Save to gallery" link after it that opens https://mobmachine.games/seeds?save=<code> in a new tab (the site saves it with sign-in), and the offer a seed link opens with: a dialog that says what the run holds and what it replaces, "Start from this seed" (the player's save is first copied to `dust.save.before-seed`) or "Keep my game". A seed never replaces a save without that choice; a broken link opens the player's own game and says so (`e2e/seed.spec.ts`). Its words are under `seed` in `content/strings.json`.
  - `render/share.ts` wires the "Share your run" button under the stage: the phone's share sheet, else the clipboard, else a plain message, with text from `share` in `content/strings.json` stating total dust, unlocks and the game's address (`tests/share.test.ts`). Where the share sheet takes files, `render/share-image.ts` adds a 1200 by 630 PNG of the run drawn in code on paper in the page's palette (dust, unlocks by name in at most two rows of pills, the address, the suit band), made synchronously so the sheet opens inside the click; a share sheet that refuses for any reason but the player dismissing it falls back to the clipboard. The button and its status line are in `index.html`.
  - `render/next-card.ts` is the Three-free next card line under the studio line: it reads the site's public documents `/api/live` and `/api/cards` (read only; `netlify.toml` proxies both paths to https://mobmachine.games, so the fetch stays on this origin under `connect-src 'self'`), picks the first card in the waterfall's funding order whose folder is seed-1, and shows its title and funding bar as one link (at least 44px tall) to its card page, https://mobmachine.games/card/<id>. No open Dust card, a failed read or a malformed document leaves the line hidden; nothing is drawn over the stage (`tests/next-card.test.ts`). Its elements are in `index.html`, its words in the module.
  - `render/whats-new.ts` is the Three-free "what's new" line above the next card line, for returning players: when a save exists under `dust.save` and an earlier visit was recorded under its own key `dust.lastVisit` (kept apart from the sim save, so `SimState` and its hash are untouched), it reads `/api/cards` and lists the seed-1 cards at stage `live` whose `live_at` is after that visit, newest first, as one link (at least 44px tall) to the newest card's page: "New in Dust since your last visit: <title>" or "N changes shipped since your last visit. Newest: <title>". The last visit moves to now once the read succeeds, or at once on a first visit. A first visit, nothing new, a failed read or a malformed document leaves it hidden (`tests/whats-new.test.ts`, `docs/specs/whats-new.md`). Its elements are in `index.html`, its words in the module.
  - `render/count.ts` counts a Dust load (`render/main.ts`), a Share your run press (`render/share.ts`) and a click from the next card line to a card (`render/next-card.ts`): it posts the event's name alone to `/api/count` on this origin, which `netlify.toml` proxies to the site's counter, with no cookie, no identifier and no address (`docs/specs/play-counts.md`; `tests/count.test.ts`). It is kernel.
  - `render/favicon.svg` is the hand-drawn tab icon, linked from `index.html` so Vite bundles it.
- `box-art.svg` is the game's box art (`docs/specs/final-state.md`, Games): a 3:4 cover drawn in code in the line style, "DUST" in thick block strokes over the island diorama, the suit band with the word "Game" and the "All ages" mark. The site shows it on the game's tile and page.
- `scripts/og-image.mjs` draws the link preview picture: a 1200 by 630 PNG on paper of the name in thick ink block strokes, a goal bar and pills, the island with its pile and flag, and the suit band, rasterised in code and encoded with `node:zlib` (no image asset, font or dependency). `scripts/postbuild.mjs` writes it to `dist/og-dust.png`, and the `og:image` and `twitter:image` tags in `index.html` name it at https://play.mobmachine.games/og-dust.png with its own alt text (`tests/og-image.test.ts`).
- Strings. The tab title the game sets once its data loads and every string the HUD, the shop and the seed offer show come from `content/strings.json`, except unit and unlock names, which come from the `config/` files. Some text lives in code instead, because it shows before `strings.json` loads, when it fails to load, or outside the canvas:
  - `index.html`: the initial `<title>`, the meta description and the Open Graph tags, the "Loading the game data." status line, the visually hidden "Dust" heading, the controls region's initial "Game controls" label, and the studio line "Made by AI agents at Mob Machine · All ages" with its link to https://mobmachine.games and "See what players are funding next" linking to https://mobmachine.games/contribute, the hidden what's new line `render/whats-new.ts` fills, the hidden next card line `render/next-card.ts` fills, the "Share your run" and "Save as seed" buttons, their space reserved and `render/share.ts` and `render/seed-ui.ts` making them visible, and the seed offer dialog with its "Seed" kicker, which `render/seed-ui.ts` fills. The page carries the all-ages label and the studio link, so it is protected: only a kernel pull request changes it.
  - `render/main.ts`: "The game data did not load. Reload the page to try again." Changing it is a code-lane card.
- `bots/` holds the greedy bot and its command line. `loadConfigFromDir(dir)` lives here because it reads the filesystem.
- `tests/` are vitest suites. `tests/fixtures/config/` is a frozen copy of the config; the pinned unlock timeline runs against the fixture, not the live config, so a config-lane card cannot break the pinned test.

## Rules of the sim

Production per second is `1 + sum(rate x milestone factor x weather on the unit x owned)` times every unlocked multiplier, the weather's whole-island multiplier, production perks and `1 + bonusPerGem x unspent gems`. A unit costs `round(baseCost x 1.25^owned)` (times the thrift perk's factor). An unlock opens when lifetime dust reaches `atTotalDust`. Gatherer is available from the start because no unlock row gates it. A strike yields one to two seconds of production times every strike multiplier (strike unlocks, the weather, strike perks).

The layers after the units are optional in the config and absent from the frozen fixture, so the pinned timeline holds (`tests/depth.test.ts` covers them):

- Milestones (`milestones` in `spawn-table.json`): owning each count in `at` multiplies that unit's rate by `factor` once more. The island shows them: gatherers wear party hats, carts and buildings fly pennants, coloured by tier.
- Unlock effects `strike` (strikes times `value`), `weather` (starts the weather) and `gems` (opens pressing the pile).
- Weather (`weather` in `unlocks.json`): from its unlock, clear spells of `gapMin` to `gapMax` whole seconds alternate with a kind picked by weight, each rolled on the seeded generator; a kind multiplies all production, strikes or named units while it lasts. `step` splits at each change, so one long step and many short ones land on the same weather. `SimState.weather` exists only once it has started.
- Gems (`gems` in `unlocks.json`), the rebirth layer: pressing the pile (`{ type: 'press' }`) gives `floor(cbrt(totalDust / dustPerGem))` gems and starts a new season: dust, units, unlocks and weather start over; gems, perks, the clock and the generator carry on. Perks (`{ type: 'perk', perk }`) cost gems once and last for good: production, strike, weather length, thrift (cheaper units) and a head start of units each season. `gems`, `perks`, `season` and `pastDust` exist in `SimState` only after the first press. The kernel bot never presses; the season bot in `tests/depth.test.ts` does.

## Lanes

A config-lane card changes values inside the `.json` files of `config/` or `content/` and nothing else: costs, rates, thresholds, names, descriptions, unlock order. Those folders hold `.json` files only; the dispatcher and the gate refuse any other file there on a config-lane card. It runs the bot and the build, not the test suite. A card that needs a new function, a new field, a new file or any change under `sim/`, `render/` or the tests is a code-lane card and runs the full gate.

## Protected paths

No agent may change these, in any lane; the dispatcher rejects the card and the gate fails a card branch that touches one (`platform/gate/kernel-paths.txt` is the list): this `CLAUDE.md`, `bots/`, `scripts/`, `index.html`, `package.json`, `netlify.toml`, `vite.config.ts`, `tsconfig.json`, `render/count.ts`, `sim/hash.ts`, `sim/rng.ts`, `sim/invariants.ts`, `tests/bot.test.ts`, `tests/invariants.test.ts`, `tests/timeline.test.ts`, `e2e/` and `playwright.config.ts`. They are the gate's harness for this game, its determinism, the frames the design review reads (the frame spec in e2e, docs/specs/design-review.md) and the page that carries the rating: only a kernel pull request changes them. The game's favicon in render is a kernel design file (`platform/gate/design-paths.txt`): no card changes it either. No agent may create or change a file with a name from `platform/gate/kernel-names.txt` either, at any depth: build, package, env and deploy configs such as a PostCSS config, a nested tsconfig, an env file or a Netlify headers file.

## Commands

From the repository root:

- `pnpm --filter @backseat/seed-1 typecheck`
- `pnpm --filter @backseat/seed-1 test`
- `pnpm --filter @backseat/seed-1 build` (typecheck, Vite build, then `scripts/postbuild.mjs` copies `config/` and `content/` into `dist/`, draws `dist/og-dust.png` and writes `dist/version.json`)
- `pnpm --filter @backseat/seed-1 bot -- --config-dir seed-1/config --hours 10 --seed 20260914 --json`

The bot resolves `--config-dir` against the directory pnpm was invoked from. It prints one JSON object with `--json` (otherwise a `PASS:`/`FAIL:` summary), exits 0 when every invariant holds, 1 when one fails or a config file does not parse, and 2 for a usage error or a config directory it cannot read. The invariants: dust never negative, every number in the state finite, at least one unlock in every completed simulated hour, and the same seed producing the same state hash twice. `--real-seconds N` bounds wall time: the first play stops after N/2 real seconds and the determinism replay repeats the same ticks in about the same time.
