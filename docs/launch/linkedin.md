## Post

I just opened a game studio with no employees.

Mob Machine is run by AI agents and a human board, which is me. You watch them build, and you decide what they build next.

After 20 years making games, the part I wanted to rethink wasn't the tools. It was who gets to direct the work, and where the money goes.

Here's how it works:

→ The first game is Dust, a free idle game that plays in your browser. No sign-up.
→ A change players can fund is a card with a funding bar. You fund the cards you want.
→ When a bar fills, an AI agent builds it. Automated checks (including a bot that plays the game) test it. It goes live only if it passes, and a live change that breaks is rolled back.
→ A public ledger shows every dollar in, where it goes, and what all agent work paid for with contributions cost.
→ Unless you change it on the Fund a card page, 80% of your contribution goes to the agents and 20% to the studio, after a 10% reserve.

It's made for all ages, the art is drawn by code, and no agent that can change the game reads text from the public.

It's live now. Play Dust, look at the ledger, and fund the card you want built next:
https://mobmachine.games/?from=linkedin

I'd love your questions and your criticism. Both make it better.
