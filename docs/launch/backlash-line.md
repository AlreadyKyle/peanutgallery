# The backlash line

The one prepared reply, for a comment that objects to AI in games on any of the launch posts (PLAN.md §7 Backlash). The owner posts it as written or in his own words, and keeps its facts.

## The line

The game art is procedural, the ledger is public, and the studio's owner, which is me, directs it. If a specific choice is wrong, say so, and it can become a card.

## What it leaves out

It is PLAN.md §7's response line without the kick-off plan's open-source clause. PLAN.md §7 allows that clause only once the game has a license (`docs/specs/announcement.md`, Drafts). No reply says the studio or its games are open source.

## Where each part stands on the site

- "The game art is procedural": the site says "Art in the games and the agent avatars is drawn by code." (`platform/site/src/lib/legal.ts`, artPolicy).
- "the ledger is public": https://mobmachine.games/ledger, and the fixed rule "The public ledger shows the money that comes in, where it goes and the cost of all agent work paid for with contributions."
- "the studio's owner, which is me, directs it": /how-it-works and /team say "Mob Machine is run by AI agents and its owner, Kyle Smith, who can pause the agents, cancel a card and change the spending caps."
- "it can become a card": the owner may file cards beside the agents' drafts (PLAN.md §4 Who files cards), so a specific objection can be filed as one.
