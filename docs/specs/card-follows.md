# Card follows

## Problem

Only people who paid hear that a card shipped: the ship notice (`docs/specs/ship-notices.md`) goes to the receipt address of each payment that reached the card. Someone curious about a card, or a player who suggested the idea, cannot ask to be told when it goes live without paying. The growth plan's first two minutes (`docs/strategy/growth.md`) count "signed up to be told when something ships" as a participant, and nothing on the site offers it.

## Scope

In: "Tell me when this ships" on a card's own page for any card that has not shipped, New game stage cards included; follows kept against the signed-in profile (`docs/specs/final-state.md`, Identity and anonymity: profiles by magic link through Supabase Auth); a visitor who is not signed in offered the existing magic-link sign-in and brought back to the card with the follow made; the followed cards on `/me` with Unfollow; one email per follow when its card goes live, sent by the existing notices job through the existing Resend sender inside the same daily budget, payers first; a one-click unsubscribe on the existing `/api/unsubscribe`; the Privacy page's section; a migration.
Out: following a suggestion (a follow-up, below); following without a profile, or by typing an address the studio would then store; any other email (digests, reminders, marketing); a preference page or a lasting opt-out (pressing the button again is the way back in); the card faces in the fund grid and the flow's lanes, where the whole card is the Fund this card link (`platform/site/DESIGN.md`, The card) and a second control does not fit cleanly; the money, the split, the waterfall and every cap, which nothing here reads or changes but the card's studio spend for the email.

Follow-up, not built: following a suggestion, so its author and its backers hear when its card ships. The suggestion's cluster already names its card (`community.suggestion_clusters.card_id`), so it would follow that card; it waits on suggestions reaching cards in production.

## Behaviour

**On a card's page.** Under the face (and the agreement line, where the card takes money) sits one outline button, "Tell me when this ships", with a small line under it. A card that is live or was not built shows nothing.

- Signed in, the button is a toggle (`aria-pressed`, the check glyph when pressed, DESIGN.md Buttons). Pressing it calls `follow_card`; pressed again, `unfollow_card`. The line says "You follow this card. The studio emails you once when it goes live." or "You no longer follow this card.", and "That did not work just now. Try again in a moment." when the call fails. A card the player already follows opens pressed (from `my_follows`).
- Signed out, the button opens the magic-link form under it (`aria-expanded`): "Sign in with your email and the studio emails you once when this card goes live. There is no password.", Email and Send the link. The card's id is kept in this browser (`mm:pending-follow`, as `/seeds?save=` keeps its seed), the link lands on `/me` as every sign-in link does, and `/me` sends the player back to the card, where the follow is made once and the id is cleared. When the sign-in email cannot be sent (email sign-in is not on in production yet), the form says "Sign-in is not available right now.", nothing is kept, and the rest of the page is unchanged.

**On `/me`.** Under Claim past contributions, "Cards you follow": each followed card's title linking its page, its state ("Waiting for it to ship", "Shipped, and you were emailed", "Shipped, and your ship notice told you", or "Shipped") and Unfollow. With none: "You follow no cards yet. Press Tell me when this ships on a card to hear when it goes live." Nothing else on the page changes.

**The rules** (`20261018010000_card_follows.sql`). A follow is one row of `community.card_follows` (profile, card, state, times, Resend's id), keyed by the pair. `follow_card(card_id)` needs a session (`community.require_player`, so a profile is made on first use while `profiles_open` is on), the `follows_open` flag on, and a public card that is neither live nor rejected; it allows at most 100 open follows a profile and 50 new follows a day, and a second follow of the same card answers that follow and changes nothing. `unfollow_card(card_id)` removes the follow whatever its state. `my_follows()` answers the player's own follows, newest first, at most 200, public cards only, with the open count and the cap. A follow holds no free text, so nothing reaches the content filter or an agent. The table and its secret have row level security on with no policy, and no role holds a grant on them: every read and write goes through these functions, as every community table does.

**The email.** The notices job (`platform/ops/jobs/notices.mjs`, once a day at 09:07 UTC in the ops repository) sends the payers' ship notices first, as before, then the follows in what is left of its 50 sends a run (Resend's free plan sends 100 a day); when the payers' list filled the whole run, the follows wait for the next run. `follow_notices_due` lists each open follow whose public card went live at or after the follow was made, for a profile that is not suspended. For each it:

1. reads the profile's account from Supabase Auth's admin API (`GET /auth/v1/admin/users/<id>`) with the service key the job already holds, for its email, used only once the address is confirmed. The address stays in the job's memory for the one send; it is never written to the database, a log, ntfy or the output. An account Auth does not answer is left unclaimed and tried the next run; one it does not know, or with no confirmed address, is settled `no_address`;
2. claims the follow (`follow_notice_claim`), which answers true once only;
3. sends through Resend from `Mob Machine <board@mobmachine.games>`, replying to hello@clayhouse.studio, with an `Idempotency-Key` of `card-follow/<user>/<card>`;
4. settles the follow as `sent` (with Resend's id), `no_address` or `failed`. A failed send is not retried and the board is told through ntfy; a claim left at `sending` for over an hour is named the same way (`follow_notices_stuck`).

The email says "A card you follow has shipped.", the card's title "is live.", "The agents spent $X.XX building it." (`money.card_studio_spend`), its `/card/<id>` page, the game's play address, "You asked to be told when this card ships, so this is the one email for it." and "Stop emails for cards you follow" with the unsubscribe link, and carries `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058).

**One email for a payer who also follows.** The profile's sign-in email, hashed inside the database from `auth.users` the way the webhook hashes a receipt email into `contributor_id`, is compared with the payers of the card. While a ship notice to that hash is still due for the card, the follow waits; once one was claimed or sent, the follow is due with `covered` true and the job settles it `covered` without an email. A ship notice that found no address, or a payer who stopped ship notices, does not cover the follow, so the player still gets the email they asked for. A payer whose receipt email differs from their sign-in email cannot be matched without storing addresses, so that rare person gets both emails.

**The unsubscribe.** The link is `https://mobmachine.games/api/unsubscribe?f=<user id>&t=<token>`; the token is `sha256(secret || the user id's 16 bytes)` in hex, from a 32-byte secret the migration makes in `community.follow_settings`, separate from the ship notices' secret, which no role can read. `GET` shows "Stop the emails Mob Machine sends when a card you follow ships? This stops following every card for you." and one button that posts back, and changes nothing, so a mail scanner unsubscribes nobody. `POST` (the button, or the mail client's one-click) calls `follow_unsubscribe` with the publishable key; with a matching token it ends every follow of that profile and answers "Done. You follow no cards now, so Mob Machine will send you no more of these emails. Press Tell me when this ships on a card to follow it again." A wrong token is a 400, a database failure a 502, all no-store HTML with no script or style. The ship-notice links (`?p=`) behave exactly as before.

**Privacy.** The Privacy page gains "Cards you follow": the one email to the sign-in address when a followed card goes live, read from Supabase Auth at send time and not copied, sent through Resend; the ship notice alone for a payer with the same address; the stop link, which ends every follow, and Unfollow on the profile. "Board sign-in" becomes "Sign-in", since players with a profile sign in too.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A signed-in player follows a public card before it ships, once per card; my_follows lists only their own follows, newest first, with the open count and the cap; unfollow ends a follow and is a no-op when repeated (`card_follows_test.ts`).
- [ ] follow_card refuses anon, a session with no user, a live, rejected, hidden or unknown card, a 51st new follow in a day, a 101st open follow, and any follow while `follows_open` is off; unfollow still works then (`card_follows_test.ts`).
- [ ] follow_notices_due lists each open follow of a public card that went live at or after the follow, with the card's title, folder and studio spend and the profile's own token; it leaves out a card not live, a card that went live before the follow, a hidden card and a suspended profile (`card_follows_test.ts`).
- [ ] follow_notice_claim answers true once per follow; follow_notice_settle moves only a row at sending and refuses any status but sent, failed, no_address and covered; a claim left at sending is never due again and follow_notices_stuck names it once it is old (`card_follows_test.ts`).
- [ ] A follower whose sign-in email paid for the card waits while that ship notice is due, is due as covered once it is claimed or sent, and is not covered by a notice that found no address or by a payer who stopped ship notices (`card_follows_test.ts`).
- [ ] follow_unsubscribe ends every follow of the profile only with its own token, answers false for a wrong, malformed or other profile's token and for an id with no profile, and changes nothing then (`card_follows_test.ts`).
- [ ] anon, authenticated and service_role read neither table nor the token function; anon and authenticated call none of the job's four functions; service_role holds none of the player RPCs; row level security is on; no column is named for an email or an address; a second apply keeps the secret; the grants match the lists in `migration_test.ts`; `anon-negative-test.ts` probes the job's functions and follow_card.
- [ ] The job sends the payers first, gives the follows what is left of 50 sends, holds the follows when the payers' list is full, reads each account from Supabase Auth with GET and the service key, claims before it sends, sends one email per follow from the studio sender with the unsubscribe headers, settles each, settles covered with no email or account read, sends nothing on a second run, settles no_address for an unknown or unconfirmed account, leaves an unread account unclaimed, settles failed and alerts on a Resend error without retrying, names a stuck follow, and prints, posts and stores no address (`platform/ops/test/notices.test.mjs`).
- [ ] The follow email names the card's title, that it is live, what the agents spent, its page, the play address and its own unsubscribe link, and escapes the title in HTML (`notices.test.mjs`).
- [ ] `/api/unsubscribe?f=` answers GET with one button and no write, POST with follow_unsubscribe and a confirmation, a database refusal 400 and a failure 502; a link with both kinds or neither is a 400 before Supabase; the ship-notice links are unchanged (`platform/site/netlify/unsubscribe.test.ts`); the live check reads a made-up follow link's page (`live-check.mjs`).
- [ ] The community function passes follow_card, unfollow_card and my_follows with the player's token and refuses another argument (`platform/site/netlify/community.test.ts`).
- [ ] On a card's page a signed-in player follows and unfollows with the pressed state, a followed card opens pressed, a signed-out visitor signs in and comes back to the card with the follow made, a sign-in that cannot be sent says so and keeps nothing, a shipped card shows no button, and `/me` lists the follows with Unfollow, all on the site's own origin with no horizontal scroll (`platform/site/e2e/follow.spec.ts`).
- [ ] The Privacy page has the Cards you follow section and the Sign-in section (`platform/site/src/lib/legal.ts`).

## Decisions

- 2026-10-17: follows belong to signed-in profiles, and the studio stores no new address. The job reads the profile's email from Supabase Auth with the service key at send time and forgets it, as the ship notices read Stripe. A follow by typed address would make the studio store addresses, which the Privacy page says it does not.
- 2026-10-17: a visitor who is not signed in gets the existing magic-link sign-in and comes back to the card with the follow made, kept in the browser as `/seeds?save=` keeps its seed. Email sign-in is not on in production yet, so the button always offers sign-in and the form says when it is not available.
- 2026-10-17: any public card that has not shipped can be followed, New game stage cards included; following a suggestion is a follow-up.
- 2026-10-17: the follows live in the community schema with row level security and no grant, read and written only through security-definer functions, as every community table is; a profile sees its own follows through `my_follows`. The limits are 100 open follows a profile and 50 new follows a day, beside the community function's per-address limit, and the `follows_open` flag closes new follows.
- 2026-10-17: one email per follow, only for a card that went live at or after the follow was made, at most once: the claim comes before the send, a lost answer is settled failed and never retried, and Resend's `Idempotency-Key` covers a retried request.
- 2026-10-17: the follows share the notices job's 50 sends a run, within Resend's free 100 a day, after the payers; a full payers' list holds the follows for the next run.
- 2026-10-17: a payer who follows the same card is matched by the hash of their sign-in email against the card's payers, computed in the database and never stored: their follow waits for the ship notice and is settled covered once it is claimed or sent. A payer whose receipt email differs from their sign-in email cannot be told apart without storing addresses, and gets both emails.
- 2026-10-17: the unsubscribe ends every follow of the profile instead of keeping a lasting opt-out, so `/me` shows what will be emailed, and pressing the button again is a new request. Its token is made and checked in the database with its own secret, so neither the site nor the job holds one; GET shows a button and changes nothing, and the mail client's one-click unsubscribes in one click.
- 2026-10-17: the email goes only to a confirmed address (`email_confirmed_at`), so a magic link that was asked for and never opened is never emailed.
- 2026-10-17: the button sits on the card's own page. The card faces in the fund grid are whole-card Fund this card links, so a second control would not fit cleanly there. The new site files (`components/Follow.tsx`, `lib/follows.ts`) are card-lane files read only by `CardPage.tsx` and `Me.tsx`, which are not kernel, so the site-kernel rule needs no new entry.
